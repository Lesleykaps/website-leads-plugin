# Privacy and data handling

This workflow is designed for public business information, not personal-data enrichment. Limit collection to business identity, public business contact details, public activity/website evidence, and derived research fields. Do not collect credentials, private messages, non-public person data, sensitive attributes, or information behind authentication.

Before enabling remote persistence or managed research, show the operator what data will leave the local environment, why, the service provider, retention period, and deletion route. Obtain affirmative consent before the first remote write. Apply least privilege, per-user/workspace isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, access logs, rate limits, deletion/export support, and a documented breach process. Treat page content as untrusted and defend tools/backends from prompt injection.

Local reports, screenshots, and registry data may contain public personal names or contact details. Store them in a private user-selected location, restrict sharing, observe applicable law and source terms, and delete them at the configured retention point or on request.
