# Evidence, confidence, and scoring

Keep source URLs, observed dates, concise observable excerpts, screenshots where required, and a sourced business-specific fact. Generic platform homepages, category/pagination pages, and generic directory listings do not qualify as business-specific evidence.

Use `High` confidence for directly observed, corroborated facts; `Medium` for credible multi-source facts with a minor unresolved detail; `Low` only for review. Qualified leads require High or Medium.

Score each lead out of 100: website problem severity (0–30), evidence strength (0–20), current activity (0–15), contactability (0–15), enquiry need (0–10), and commercial fit (0–10). `High` priority is 80–100 with High confidence; `Strong` is 60–79 or Medium confidence; `Possible` is 40–59. Scores below 40 ordinarily go to review.

For a batch of 10+ assessed candidates, audit a cross-industry sample of at least three or 10%, whichever is larger, if at least 80% share a status or exact total. Use a new browser session and an independent check. If it does not reproduce the defect, move that cluster to review and record the audit.
