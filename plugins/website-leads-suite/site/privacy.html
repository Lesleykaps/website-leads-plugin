Privacy Policy

What Website Leads Suite handles

Website Leads Suite is designed to research publicly available business information: business identity, public business contact details, website and activity evidence, screenshots, derived research notes, and operator-created draft outreach. It is not designed for personal-data enrichment or private-source collection.

How data is used

Information is used to create an auditable research report and, if requested, a review-ready campaign for the operator. The skills-only version stores no data with Cipher Technologies. Local reports, campaign records, and optional local deduplication data remain where the operator chooses to store them.

Optional connected services

If an operator enables a separate team registry, managed research service, or email provider connection, that service must explain its provider, data categories, retention, deletion route, and access controls before the first remote write. Its own privacy notice applies. The plugin does not bundle email credentials or an email account.

Retention and security

Operators should restrict access to research reports and campaign records, use a retention period appropriate to their purpose, and delete local data when no longer needed. Do not place credentials, tokens, or private communications in Website Leads Suite records.

Your choices

You can delete local run data and any local registry from the configured data location. For questions about this policy, contact Cipher Technologies at info@ciphertechnologies.co.zw.
