Evidence-backed prospect workflow

Research leads, prepare honest outreach, and keep control of every send.

Website Leads Suite verifies observable website issues, public business evidence, and duplicates; it then creates review-ready drafts. Delivery is optional and requires your own authorized email provider and explicit approval.

View the plugin

Evidence first

Every qualified lead has business-specific sources, a factual issue summary, confidence, scores, and screenshots for existing-site findings.

Approval before delivery

Drafting never contacts anyone. Delivery only considers rows marked Approved, through an account you connect and authorize.

Portable by design

Your sender identity and private deduplication history stay in your own configuration; no personal account is bundled.

How it works

  1. Research public business evidence and produce a four-sheet XLSX report.
  2. Create an evidence-grounded review workbook and outreach drafts.
  3. Optionally preflight or deliver explicitly approved rows through your own provider.
