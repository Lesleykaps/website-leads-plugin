Privacy Policy

What Website Leads handles

Website Leads is designed to research publicly available business information: business identity, public business contact details, website and activity evidence, screenshots, and derived research notes. It is not designed for personal-data enrichment or private-source collection.

How data is used

Information is used to create an auditable research report for the operator. The skills-only version stores no data with Website Leads Contributors. Local reports and optional local deduplication data remain where the operator chooses to store them.

Optional connected services

If an operator later enables a separate team registry or managed research service, that service must explain its provider, data categories, retention, deletion route, and access controls before the first remote write. Its own privacy notice applies.

Retention and security

Operators should restrict access to research reports, use a retention period appropriate to their purpose, and delete reports and local registry data when no longer needed. Do not place credentials or private communications in Website Leads records.

Your choices

You can delete local run data and any local registry from the configured data location. For questions about this policy, contact the publisher at the support address shown in the plugin listing.
